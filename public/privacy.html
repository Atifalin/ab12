Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

AB12 ("the app") is a free, ad-free learning game for toddlers and preschoolers, made for children and their families. This policy explains what little data the app touches — the short answer is: almost none.

What we collect

  • No personal information. We do not ask for names, emails, accounts, or any sign-up.
  • No ads and no tracking cookies. There are no third-party advertisers, no ad networks, and no cross-site tracking.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. To know which games are loved, the app sends anonymous counters (for example, "a phone session started" or "the puzzle game was played for 2 minutes"). These contain no identifiers and cannot be tied to you or your child.
  • On-device storage. Game progress and settings (like chosen voices and backgrounds) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.

Speech and audio

Letter and word pronunciation uses your device's built-in speech synthesis and pre-recorded audio files. Audio is generated or played locally on your device; we do not record or store anything you say.

Children's privacy

AB12 is designed for children ages 1–5. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone — child or adult — it complies with the spirit of children's privacy rules such as COPPA. If you have questions, contact the site owner through the domain administrator.

Third parties

The app loads the Fredoka font from Google Fonts, which may receive standard request data (like IP address) under Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party services are used.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
