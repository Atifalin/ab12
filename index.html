About AB12 — what’s inside & FAQ

What is AB12?

AB12 is a free, ad-free learning playground for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 1–5). It combines alphabet and number practice, phonics, spelling, puzzles and animal sounds into 11 big-button games that work with taps or a keyboard — no reading required to start playing.

The 11 games inside AB12

  • Learn — Letters & Numbers, Word Monsters phonics and Spell it! build ABCs, 123s and first words.
  • Think — Picture Puzzle, Sort it! and Feed the Monster build matching, sorting and counting skills.
  • Play — Smash!, Animal Farm and Build & Go! are pure energetic fun with sounds and surprises.
  • Family — Peekaboo! turns family photos into a personalized game kids love.

Frequently asked questions

Is AB12 free?

Yes — completely free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no account or sign-up.

What ages is it for?

It’s designed for toddlers and preschoolers, roughly ages 1 to 5.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes — it runs in any modern browser on iPhone, iPad, Android and desktop, and can be added to the home screen like an app.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes — no ads, no chat, no external links inside the games and no personal data collection. An optional parent lock protects the settings. See the privacy policy.
